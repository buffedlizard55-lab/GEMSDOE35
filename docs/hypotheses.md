# Geological hypotheses and evidence register

**Updated:** 2026-10-04 UTC. This register separates scientifically motivated hypotheses from measured DTI results, public leaderboard observations, and owner-reported claims. “Not tried” below means the exact detector/signature is not implemented in this checkout; it is not a claim of global novelty. Sibling-site histories are not a complete, independently audited parameter ledger.

## Target and rules that constrain the science

- The official [problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) says that the initial private test set contains expert-identified fault pixels absent from the current public USGS map; test-source details are not disclosed. A local holdout on public mapped faults is therefore a **proxy**, not a simulation of the private target.
- DrivenData staff define a new fault pixel as geometry not already represented by USGS/INGENIOUS, including a continuation or splay of an existing fault system ([definition, forum topic 11536/post 2](https://community.drivendata.org/t/where-do-you-draw-the-line/11536/2)). Scoring masks catalogue pixels **exactly**; nearby predictions are scored normally, and new geometry may occur within 300 m of known traces ([clarification, topic 11516/post 4](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4)). No detector below uses a catalogue-distance buffer.
- A geophysical anomaly cannot by itself establish that its associated fault is absent from a catalogue. The scientifically defensible claim is narrower: the signature may prioritize a candidate trace/continuation for expert mapping. This checkout's owner-mirror labels support only spatial transfer tests on known faults.
- Training features/labels/template presently come from a public owner-maintained mirror. Pinned hashes establish mirror consistency only; they do not authenticate organizer provenance or licensing. The official competition data page remains login-gated in this environment.

### H35-03 preregistration and promotion gate

Before writing its detector, the eight-row mixed LHS in [`configs/h35-03-lhs.json`](../configs/h35-03-lhs.json) was registered. The five numeric dimensions are maximum Gaussian support scale (200–1,000 m), flank-curvature weight (0–1), conductance weight (0–0.8), gravity-edge weight (0–0.8), and prediction fraction (0.4%–1.2%). `mag_anom`, RTP and TMI are balanced categorical levels (three, three and two configurations). Three filter scales, the baseline, catalogue-mask convention, spatial split, margin, holdout-reuse caveat and metric support are fixed and included in each configuration ID. The sampler must put one candidate in each stratum of every numeric dimension; this is marginal stratification, not a guarantee of interaction coverage or gain.

Validation uses nested leave-one-tile-out selection over a 3×2 spatial grid. A 3×3 grid was rejected because two corners have no valid footprint and another has only 153 positive pixels. A 4×2 alternative left one core with just 43,353 valid and 654 positive pixels, so the 3×2 layout was selected: all six cores retain at least 132,345 valid and 2,296 positive pixels after the 30-pixel margin. For each outer tile, select one of the eight configurations using DTI deltas on the other five tiles, then score that selected configuration and the current H35-01 candidate against the `tmi_hg` baseline on the held-out tile. Re-emit all comparison arms at the same actual pixel mass. Each tile has a 30-pixel (3-km) edge margin, separating evaluation cores from adjacent development cores. The predeclared promotion gate requires positive mean outer-fold DTI delta and at least 5/6 positive outer tiles **against both** `tmi_hg` and H35-01, with matched emitted mass in every fold. No TIFF is written on failure.

**Independence warning:** H35-01/H35-02 already reported aggregate scores over the four coarse quadrants, exposing label geography within every planned tile (the middle 3×2 row also crosses the earlier north/south split). The nested selection keeps each outer tile's labels out of its own configuration selection in this run, but the geography is not a pristine untouched replication after historical analysis. Report H35-03 as exploratory spatial CV, not independent confirmation, a private-test estimate or a public leaderboard prediction. No competition slot is spent automatically. A passing local gate only makes the candidate eligible for manual review.

## H35-03 local screen result — gate failed, no new TIFF

The frozen eight-row mixed LHS (seed 20261004; five numeric factors and balanced `mag_anom`/RTP/TMI counts of 3/3/2) was evaluated on the registered 3×2 nested spatial design. Every comparison arm was re-emitted at the same actual pixel count on each outer tile. The mean outer-fold DTI delta was **+0.005324 vs `tmi_hg`** (4/6 folds positive), but **−0.002998 vs H35-01** (3/6 positive); all six folds had exact matched emissions. The promotion gate required a positive mean and at least 5/6 positive folds against both comparators, so it **failed**. No H35-03 TIFF was produced and no competition slot was used.

After computing outer-fold results, the all-tile diagnostic selection was `h35-03-9388e69e81` (RTP; max scale 462.30 m; flank-curvature weight 0.4798; conductance weight 0.0150; gravity-edge weight 0.4877; prediction fraction 1.1168%). This all-tile selected configuration is not an untouched score estimate. The outer-fold selection mostly chose it but did not beat the frozen H35-01 incumbent consistently. Full per-tile/design results and hashes: [`reports/h35-03-20261004T185851097813Z-9388e69e81.json`](../reports/h35-03-20261004T185851097813Z-9388e69e81.json), [latest H35-03 evidence JSON](evidence/h35-03-latest.json), and [`reports/h35-03-latest.json`](../reports/h35-03-latest.json). The carry-over H35-01 TIFF remains the only download; it is not replaced by this failed challenger.

The six 3×2 cores all had positive truth support (minimum 2,296 pixels), but area counts differ and prior H35-01/H35-02 quadrant summaries exposed their label geography. Interpret this as exploratory proxy evidence only. These data do **not** retire any registered LHS dimension or establish how the detector performs on newly identified private-target faults.

## Previous implemented tests and their results

### H35-01 — wavelength-persistent magnetic contacts

H35-01 combines upward-continuation RTP/TMI gradient persistence with orientation coherence and optional cover-depth/gravity/slope corroboration. It was tested in eight LHS configurations against a matched-mass single-scale `tmi_hg` ranking on NE/SW/SE development folds, then NW. On the selected candidate, the archived NW DTI was **0.026904** vs **0.016573** for `tmi_hg` (Δ **+0.010331**) at 23,427 predicted pixels; development mean Δ was **+0.014452**, positive on all three blocks. This is a public-catalogue proxy result on the unverified owner mirror, not a private/public leaderboard score. The TIF is kept as a candidate, not an organizer-scored submission: [report](../reports/h35-01-20261004T164802Z-e58e5dbee6.json), [validation receipt](../reports/submission_validation.json), [TIF](downloads/gemsdoe35-h35-01-e58e5dbee6-20261004T164802Z-candidate.tif).

### H35-02 — multiscale strain-field discontinuities

H35-02 used `geod_2ndinv`, `geod_shearrate` and `geod_dilaterate` to score smoothed gradient magnitude/orientation rather than hotspots. Its LHS selected dilatation rate; mean development Δ vs `tmi_hg` was **+0.018067**, but only 2/3 blocks were positive. On reused NW it scored 0.016990 vs 0.019426 for `tmi_hg`. At the selected 1.1976% budget, H35-01 was rescored fairly at **0.030867**; each of the four candidate/baseline arms emitted 27,979 pixels. H35-02 therefore failed both its all-development-fold gate and comparison to the exact current incumbent; **no H35-02 TIFF was emitted**. The NW comparison was reused, not an independent confirmation. The earlier unequal-budget report is superseded. See the [corrected report](../reports/h35-02-20261004T180550Z-6882a35675.json) and [experiment protocol](design-of-experiments.md#h35-02-challenger-strain-field-discontinuities).

## Public score observations and the reported H33 value

A manually reviewed, date-stamped official [public leaderboard snapshot from 2026-10-04](evidence/leaderboard-20261004.json) lists `nchuzhoy` at **0.3262** (rank 1), `kinghorton42` at **0.3222** (rank 2), `DARD` at **0.3195** (rank 3), and `extradr19` at **0.2778** (rank 13). It is not a private or final prize score, and the board is a moving public subset. The user-supplied statement that 0.3195 was the highest is inconsistent with that dated snapshot; live automation is intentionally not performed under the current DrivenData Terms of Use.

The attribution “H33 got 0.2778” is **not verified**. The official public row carrying 0.2778 is `extradr19`; the available evidence does not link that row to an H33 GeoTIFF or submission ID. The owner-maintained [GEMSDOE32 page](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/GEMSDOE32/docs/index.html) and [H33 PR #9](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/pull/9) describe H33-2-B2 as a **projected live 0.2747 model, not a score**, and state that no organizer score exists for those artifacts. Therefore the mechanism behind the public 0.2778 entry cannot be attributed to H33 from verified evidence.

The H33 page/PR describe a catalogue-flank pruning idea, but it must not be copied as an unverified shortcut. Mechanistically, removing a prediction can help DTI if the false-positive penalty avoided exceeds the true-positive credit lost. That hypothetical tradeoff needs evidence from labels matching the competition target; the visible known-fault catalogue cannot establish it, and nearby predictions are not excluded by the official scorer. A higher score than 0.2778 is certainly possible because the same dated public board already has higher rows; beating 0.3262 or winning either prize round is unknown. No local DTI number is converted into a leaderboard estimate.
