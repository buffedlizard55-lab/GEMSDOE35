## Irregularities and blockers

1. **Organizer data unavailable here:** official data tab requires enrolled-account access. No login bypass or credential automation is used. Public mirror checks verify mirror consistency only.
2. **Owner mirror differs from official description:** its sample raster values match known labels although the public description says the sample predicts total fault absence; sample values are not used for predictions.
3. **Feature nodata:** 3,061 sentinel cells per selected feature band occur inside the local finite scoring footprint. The loader masks and nearest-fills them; their influence has not been isolated.
4. **Invalidated catalogue-buffer experiment:** a first H35-01 draft suppressed a 0–3 px region, was invalidated after staff clarified exact-pixel masking, and did not produce a current candidate.
5. **Leaderboard and H33 attribution:** 0.2778 is on the date-stamped public board for `extradr19`; H33's association is unverified and owner materials explicitly label H33-2-B2 unscored.
6. **Proxy/target mismatch:** current holdouts use existing public catalogue faults, while the initial private evaluation is based on newly identified fault pixels. No score translation is justified.
7. **Radiometric external data not locally inspected:** official USGS release, CC0 1.0 rights statement, Area 2 TIFF zip, byte size, MD5 and catalog download URI are verified in ScienceBase metadata. The archive was not transferred to this workspace; extracted channels, actual CRS/transform, target overlap and use under competition rules remain unverified.
8. **MT CRS/availability mismatch:** USGS ScienceBase extension metadata says EPSG:4269, FGDC describes a custom Albers 1-km grid, and the direct binary could not be retrieved from this sandbox. Do not use until resolved.
9. **Historical holdout exposure:** the 3×2 H35-03 nested folds fall within the geography of previously reported coarse-quadrant summaries, with the middle row crossing the old north/south split. Each outer fold is excluded from its own LHS selection in the registered run, but this remains exploratory, not pristine independent confirmation.
10. **No automatic leaderboard feed:** current Terms prevent an autonomous scraper without consent/API. Maintain a date-stamped manually reviewed snapshot.
11. **Submission is manual:** the code can build and validate a GeoTIFF but does not access DrivenData or consume a weekly slot. The current H35-05 TIFF is experimental and failed the incumbent gate; do not submit it on this evidence. Official authentication, file provenance review and user eligibility must be checked by an enrolled competitor.
12. **H35-05 seismicity layers and screen:** `ieq_n100a15`/`deq_n100a15` descriptions are from the owner mirror, not verified organizer metadata; the surfaces may be correlated. H35-05 failed its nested matched-mass local proxy gate (−0.011534 mean vs H35-01; 2/6 positive). Its experimental TIFF is format-checked and locally unique only within the available repository scan. No score is organizer-verified and no submission slot was used.
