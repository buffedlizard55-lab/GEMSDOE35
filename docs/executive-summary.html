Manual DrivenData workflow

How to make the submission.

Confirm account and eligibility

Sign in to the official GEMS Prize page, enroll, and review the current rules/eligibility requirements. Do not share credentials with this project.

Review data provenance

Compare against files available through your enrolled account. This artifact uses an owner mirror, not organizer-authenticated bytes; if your official grid/data differ, regenerate from your authorized files rather than uploading blindly.

Open the submit form

On DrivenData, choose the competition's Submit action, then Make new submission. Upload the `.tif` directly as the single GeoTIFF prediction file; do not upload a report or rename an old submission.

Name and identify it

Use `GEMSDOE35-H35-01-e58e5dbee6` and paste the short note above if the form offers a description/note field; otherwise keep it with your local entry record. Check the site response and retain the final organizer receipt/score separately.
