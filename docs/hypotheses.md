# Geological hypotheses and verified evidence register

**Registered:** 2026-10-04 UTC, before detector implementation.<br>
**Current-checkout audit:** this checkout began as a README-only repository: no previous model, transforms, experiment ledger, holdout result, or submission TIFF were present. Public sibling-repository pages are owner-maintained research reports, not organizer verification; they show adjacent work in several of these families, so novelty is limited to the exact tests described below, not a claim of global first use.

## Evidence and target definition

- **Official competition page:** the initial private test labels are expert-identified faults not in the current public USGS database. The supplied training layers and known fault labels come from USGS and the Great Basin Center for Geothermal Energy's INGENIOUS project. The public page does not disclose the private test sources/types/coverage; DrivenData staff confirmed that limitation in [forum topic 11527, post 7](https://community.drivendata.org/t/how-were-the-new-test-faults-identified-data-sources-and-fault-types/11527/7).
- **Important organizer clarification:** a “new fault” means any fault pixel not already captured by USGS/INGENIOUS and can include a newly mapped continuation, splay, or other geometry of an existing system ([staff reply, topic 11536, post 2](https://community.drivendata.org/t/where-do-you-draw-the-line/11536/2)). Known catalogue pixels are masked **pixel-exactly** in scoring; nearby predictions are not given a free buffer, and a new-fault pixel may lie within 300 m of a known trace ([staff reply, topic 11516, post 4](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4)). Therefore this repository never dilates the known-fault mask in its final candidate.
- **Owner-mirror caveat:** the local 19-band TIFF, label raster, and sample raster match the sibling mirror's published manifest hashes, but that establishes only mirror consistency—not organizer provenance. The official data tab remains login-walled here. The sample raster equals labels on its finite footprint, despite the official page describing a total-fault-absence sample; its values are used only for grid/footprint checks, never as prediction values.
- **Prior-art caveat:** sibling project pages report experiments with magnetic edges/ridges, strain, seismicity, topography, DEM, thermal/geochemical and MT/clay-cap layers, output sparsity, and catalogue buffers. Exact full code/config histories are not available for independent audit. “Untried” below means not present in this checkout at session start and/or a narrower new signature than the named reported family—not proven absent from every team or repository.

### Why this order

H35-01 uses available feature bands and directly tests buried-contact persistence with manageable computation. H35-02 is cheaper but risks being too smooth. H35-03 has a plausible hydrothermal mechanism but a high lithology/alteration confound. H35-04 adds independent depth information but the official regional product is roughly 1 km and cannot be assumed to resolve 100 m traces. These are test priorities, not evidence of superior DTI. The sponsor says it is not disclosing private test data sources, types, or coverage, so no public feature can be asserted to match that private population.

## H35-01 design and evidence status

The design factors and mixed LHS are in [`configs/h35-01-lhs.json`](../configs/h35-01-lhs.json); protocol/rationale are in [`design-of-experiments.md`](design-of-experiments.md). An initial experiment draft varied and applied a 0–3 pixel known-fault buffer, based on prior owner reports. It was **invalidated** after the organizer's exact-pixel-mask clarification and must not be used as evidence or submitted. The corrected v2 LHS fixes the mask to exact known-label pixels only and is the only valid experiment. Results are written to `reports/`; a candidate TIFF is emitted only if the three development folds and frozen NW spatial holdout all beat the single-scale `tmi_hg` incumbent. Even then it remains a local catalogue-label proxy, not hidden-test evidence.
