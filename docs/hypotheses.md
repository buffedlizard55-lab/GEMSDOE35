# Geological hypotheses and verified evidence register

**Registered:** 2026-10-04 UTC, before detector implementation.<br>
**Current-checkout audit:** this checkout began as a README-only repository: no previous model, transforms, experiment ledger, holdout result, or submission TIFF were present. Public sibling-repository pages are owner-maintained research reports, not organizer verification; they show adjacent work in several of these families, so novelty is limited to the exact tests described below, not a claim of global first use.

## Evidence and target definition

- **Official competition page:** the initial private test labels are expert-identified faults not in the current public USGS database. The supplied training layers and known fault labels come from USGS and the Great Basin Center for Geothermal Energy's INGENIOUS project. The public page does not disclose the private test sources/types/coverage; DrivenData staff confirmed that limitation in [forum topic 11527, post 7](https://community.drivendata.org/t/how-were-the-new-test-faults-identified-data-sources-and-fault-types/11527/7).
- **Important organizer clarification:** a “new fault” means any fault pixel not already captured by USGS/INGENIOUS and can include a newly mapped continuation, splay, or other geometry of an existing system ([staff reply, topic 11536, post 2](https://community.drivendata.org/t/where-do-you-draw-the-line/11536/2)). Known catalogue pixels are masked **pixel-exactly** in scoring; nearby predictions are not given a free buffer, and a new-fault pixel may lie within 300 m of a known trace ([staff reply, topic 11516, post 4](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4)). Therefore this repository never dilates the known-fault mask in its final candidate.
- **Owner-mirror caveat:** the local 19-band TIFF, label raster, and sample raster match the sibling mirror's published manifest hashes, but that establishes only mirror consistency—not organizer provenance. The official data tab remains login-walled here. The sample raster equals labels on its finite footprint, despite the official page describing a total-fault-absence sample; its values are used only for grid/footprint checks, never as prediction values.
- **Prior-art caveat:** sibling project pages report experiments with magnetic edges/ridges, strain, seismicity, topography, DEM, thermal/geochemical and MT/clay-cap layers, output sparsity, and catalogue buffers. Exact full code/config histories are not available for independent audit. “Untried” below means not present in this checkout at session start and/or a narrower new signature than the named reported family—not proven absent from every team or repository.

## Ranked hypotheses (pre-implementation)

### Why this order and current H35-02 status

H35-01 used available feature bands and directly tested buried-contact persistence with manageable computation. H35-02 was screened next because it was relatively inexpensive and uses supplied layers, but its derivatives may be too smooth. H35-03 has a plausible hydrothermal mechanism but a high lithology/alteration confound. H35-04 adds independent depth information, but its official record reports native CRS EPSG:4269 while the exact native pixel size, target coverage, and alignment still require a local raster audit; do not infer 100 m line detail before checking the files. These are test priorities, not evidence of superior DTI. The sponsor says it is not disclosing private test data sources, types, or coverage, so no public feature can be asserted to match that private population.

**H35-02 screen result (2026-10-04 UTC):** failed the preregistered promotion gate. The selected strain-dilatation edge setting had mean development ΔDTI +0.018067 vs `tmi_hg`, but only 2/3 development blocks were positive; on the reused NW block it scored 0.016990, below the 0.019426 `tmi_hg` baseline. H35-01 was rescored at H35-02's selected fraction (1.1976%): it scored 0.030867 versus H35-02's 0.016990 with exactly 27,979 predictions in each arm (Δ −0.013877). The historic H35-01 score 0.026904 used 23,427 predictions and must not be compared directly. No H35-02 TIFF was emitted. This does not prove all strain-derived hypotheses fail; it closes only this specified LHS screening batch. Details and reuse caveat are in [`design-of-experiments.md`](design-of-experiments.md#h35-02-challenger-strain-field-discontinuities) and the [corrected machine report](../reports/h35-02-20261004T180550Z-6882a35675.json).

## Public score observations and the reported H33 value

The official public leaderboard was read on **2026-10-04 UTC**. It showed `nchuzhoy` at **0.3262** (rank 1), `kinghorton42` at **0.3222** (rank 2), and `DARD` at **0.3195** (rank 3). The same page showed a public **0.2778** at rank 13 for `extradr19`. These are moving **public leaderboard** scores, not the private initial-round score or final prize-round outcome; the official problem page describes separate private-label scoring and later expanded-label rescoring.

The reported association “H33 scored 0.2778” is **not verified**. A public score of 0.2778 exists, but the visible participant is `extradr19`; an H33 artifact-to-submission linkage was not found. The owner-maintained [GEMSDOE32 H33 PR #9](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/pull/9) documents a removal/pruning arm and calls H33-2-B2 a **projected live 0.2747 (a model, not a score)**; the PR explicitly says no organizer score exists for its artifacts. This is the verified limit of the attribution.

**Possible mechanism, not a score attribution:** a removal arm can improve a Tversky-style score when the false-positive cost of removed pixels exceeds the true-positive credit lost. H33's owner-reported local analysis treated a near-catalogue “flank prune” as promising, then projected H33-2-B2 at 0.2747 from a local model. Neither that projection nor the public 0.2778 identifies how the `extradr19` score was achieved. We do **not** transplant the buffer rule: organizers clarify that only catalogue pixels are masked exactly, nearby predictions receive ordinary penalties, and newly identified geometry may fall near known traces. A public score greater than 0.2778 is plainly plausible because the current board already has entries above it, but surpassing 0.3262—or the eventual private/final outcome—is not established. No local DTI delta is a leaderboard estimate.

## H35-01 design and evidence status

The design factors and mixed LHS are in [`configs/h35-01-lhs.json`](../configs/h35-01-lhs.json); protocol/rationale are in [`design-of-experiments.md`](design-of-experiments.md). An initial experiment draft varied and applied a 0–3 pixel known-fault buffer, based on prior owner reports. It was **invalidated** after the organizer's exact-pixel-mask clarification and must not be used as evidence or submitted. The corrected v2 LHS fixes the mask to exact known-label pixels only and is the only valid experiment. Results are written to `reports/`; a candidate TIFF is emitted only if the three development folds and frozen NW spatial holdout all beat the single-scale `tmi_hg` incumbent. Even then it remains a local catalogue-label proxy, not hidden-test evidence.
